# Business definitions 

In all the Case Study there is a context, what is called in Computer Science "business". 

This year, the context is "Visionary Studios", a creative design company. 

For that we need to have some general ideas of how they work and some lexicon. 

# Brief process

In a design company you usually don't want to design for yourself, you design for _others_. So usually you will have a company and you will have clients. It's similar to the process of the design that you have experienced in the IA but slightly different. 

Let's imagine that you have "Visionary Studios" and one day the Municipality knocks on your door to do a small campaign for the main festivities.  (I'm going to use Mataró festivities: "Les Santes" as an example)

You meet the client (maybe the mayor if it's a small town but likely some other people from the administration) and you discuss what are the details. 

* **The project**. Is it an advertising campaign? a rebranding? what is the context of this project? what are the limits? In the example the imaginery for "Les Santes"
* **The goals**. In the example, what are the values that are behind that the imaginery needs to stand out.
* **The deliverables** The final elements that are needed. Maybe it's a poster. Maybe it's a poster and a full video, and merchandising or is material for a concert. In this case let's supose that can be the poster, the layout for the program of Les Santes and the design for a T-Shirt and many others.

![](https://capgros.elnacional.cat/uploads/s1/13/82/67/1/dscf7646_7_960x634.jpeg)

The **brief** (or briefing) is the name of the meeting and the document where this is detailed. It's quite important. 

Usually, in this creative process there is a stage of **drafts** where the company sends different sketched ideas. Following the les Santes scenario you can go with different ideas. These drafts are usually not finished but represent different approaches. For example, even if they are finished works you can imagine that the drafts of these posters have been wildly different:

Once the draft is approved and received feedback, then production starts. The finals (or deliverables) are done and usually with another session of smaller feedback of adjustment. This process may be repeated when there is derivative work once the main piece is done (in the example of les Santes, the poster is the main one, then you create the t-shirt, the vases, etc). 

## Where to use AI? 

There are several parts where AI is used here. For example, for doing the sketches. Problem: AI is going to do an _average generic thing_. But some clients want the "finished look" of AI. 

Also AI can be used for the actual production. Problem: kind of complicated do adjust properly. If the deliverable is a vector, it's needed another type of generation or another step for vectorizing.
